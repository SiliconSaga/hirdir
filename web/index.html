How this works

Getting going. Open "Setup and export" and load your team file. Tap a kid on the bench to put them on the field — the clock starts with the first one.

Subbing. While there's room, a tap puts a kid straight on. Once the field is full, tapping a bench kid asks who comes off, already suggesting whoever has been on longest. Tap a different name on the field to swap someone else out instead.

If you lose track — and you will — tap Roll call and tap everyone who is actually out there. That becomes the truth; nobody loses their minutes.

Undo takes back the last thing you did, a whole substitution included.

The buttons on a kid's row

Scored a goal. The number beside it is their tally.

Doing great — you can spend your attention elsewhere.
shy
Hanging back, needs drawing in.
help
Struggling, needs you.
away
Not here today. They drop out of the fair-share maths and move to "Away today", where a tap brings them back if they turn up late.

★, shy and help are toggles — tap again to clear. They're notes to yourself for next season's team-picking, not a judgement on anyone.

The numbers

On the field: how long this stint has run, then total minutes today.

On the bench: total minutes today, plus "owed 2" once a kid is a minute or more behind an even share. The owed figure grows the longer they sit, and the bench is sorted by it — so the kid at the top is always the one to send on next. Red says the same thing louder.

An even share is the game so far × players per side ÷ kids here. It grows every second, which is why a kid who has just come off shows no figure and picks one up after a minute on the bench.

5/5 in the corner is who's on versus how many you play. If it turns red, the app thinks a different number is out there than you do — roll call fixes it.

Ending up

Pause is halftime; nobody accrues time while it's paused. At full time, "End game", then Export game before you close the tab — that's the record.

Everything stays on this phone. Nothing about the kids is sent anywhere.
